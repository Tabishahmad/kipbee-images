KipBee Hub – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

KipBee Hub is a private, internal tool used only by KipBee (New Delhi, India) to manage its own seller and advertising accounts on Amazon.in. It is not offered to the public or to other sellers.

What data we access

With our own authorization, the tool reads and updates data of KipBee's own accounts through Amazon's official APIs: product listings, prices, stock, orders, and advertising campaigns, keywords, bids and performance reports.

How we use it

  • Only to manage KipBee's own listings, inventory and advertising.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not use any data for profiling or for any purpose outside managing our own business.

Customer personal data

Customer information (such as names or addresses on orders) is used only to fulfil those orders, is never shared, and is not kept longer than needed to fulfil the order and meet legal or tax requirements.

Storage and security

Data is stored on KipBee's own computer. Access keys are kept in a private configuration file, are never published, and can be revoked at any time from the Amazon account.
